ABOUT US

A Delhi Food Tradition

Sita Ram Diwan Chand is known for its iconic chole bhature and its long-standing connection with Delhi's food culture.

A legacy built around one unforgettable dish.

The story of Sita Ram Diwan Chand began with Shri Sita Ram Ji, who started selling chole bhature in Delhi in 1950.

Over the decades, the family business grew while continuing to focus on the traditional flavours that made it popular with generations of customers.

Today, the Paharganj outlet continues to serve its signature chole bhature in the heart of one of Delhi's most recognizable food destinations.
