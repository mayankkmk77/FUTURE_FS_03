# Sita Ram Diwan Chand — Local Business Website

A responsive local-business website created for **Sita Ram Diwan Chand, Paharganj, New Delhi**, showcasing its food heritage, signature chole bhature, menu highlights, gallery, location, and contact information.

## About the Project

This project was developed as part of the **Future Interns Full Stack Web Development Internship — Task 3: Local Business Website & Live Pitch**.

The goal was to create a professional and responsive online presence for a real local business using standard frontend technologies.

The website presents important information in a simple, visually appealing format so that potential customers can easily:

* Learn about the restaurant
* Explore its food offerings
* View food and restaurant imagery
* Find the restaurant's location
* Check opening hours
* Call the restaurant
* Get directions through Google Maps
* Visit the official website
* View current public customer reviews

## Business Growth Pitch

### How This Website Helps the Business

A strong online presence can make it easier for customers to discover a local restaurant before visiting.

This website helps **Sita Ram Diwan Chand** by:

* **Improving online visibility** through a dedicated business website
* **Showcasing the restaurant's identity** and long-standing food heritage
* **Making important information easily accessible**, including address, phone number and opening hours
* **Helping customers discover the menu and food offerings** before visiting
* **Providing direct navigation** through Google Maps
* **Making the restaurant easier to contact** with click-to-call functionality
* **Building trust** by directing visitors to public customer reviews and the official website
* **Providing a mobile-friendly experience** for customers searching while travelling or exploring Paharganj

The website therefore acts as a simple digital storefront that helps potential customers discover the business, learn about its offerings, and take action to visit or contact the restaurant.
